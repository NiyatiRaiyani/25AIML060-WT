About Student Hub

CHARUSAT Student Hub is a web portal developed for students of CSPIT, Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning. It provides easy access to academic and departmental information.

Our Mission

To provide a simple, secure, and user-friendly platform for students to access important resources and services.

Our Vision

To build a smart digital learning environment that supports innovation, collaboration, and student success.

What We Offer

  • Student Registration
  • Secure Login
  • Department Notices
  • Study Materials
  • Event Information
  • Student Dashboard
  • Profile Management
  • Feedback Support
